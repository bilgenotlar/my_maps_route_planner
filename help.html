🤔Why does this extension exist?

You've spent hours planning a trip on Google My Maps — layers for each day, marked places, drawn routes. But when you pull out your phone on the road, Google Maps mobile can't open your My Maps plan for navigation. You end up re-searching every place from scratch.

😤 The Problem
Google My Maps is a planning tool. Google Maps is a navigation tool. There is no built-in bridge between them.
✅ The Solution
This extension builds that bridge. It reads the KML data from your open My Maps page and automatically generates shareable Google Maps navigation links.

🛠️Installation

1
Download the ZIP and extract it to a permanent folder
Do not delete this folder — Chrome loads the extension from it continuously.
2
Enable Developer Mode in Chrome
Type chrome://extensions in the address bar and toggle on Developer mode in the top-right corner.
3
Click "Load unpacked" and select the folder
The extension icon appears in your toolbar. If hidden, click the puzzle icon (🧩) and pin it.
⚠️ When updating: remove the extension first, then re-add it. New permissions only activate after a fresh install.

📱Step-by-step usage

On Desktop — Planning

1
Build your plan in My Maps
Go to google.com/maps/d/ and create a layer for each day or theme. Add place markers (📍 Places) or directions routes (🛣️ Route).
2
Open the extension
Click the extension icon on the My Maps tab. Layers are read automatically and the map name appears in the popup header.
3
Customize your route (optional)
Use ▲ ▼ to reorder stops. Click 📝 next to any stop or place to add a note (max 100 characters).
4
Select travel mode
Choose 🚗 / 🚶 / 🚌 / 🚲 on each route card. The navigation link updates instantly.
5
Get your links and share
Use Copy, WhatsApp, QR, or open directly. Use 📋 Copy all in the bottom bar to grab every route at once.

On Mobile — During the Trip

Tap the link or scan the QR code. Google Maps opens with the correct place names ready to navigate.

Name in My Maps What Google Maps shows A → Grand Anzac Hotel = A → Grand Anzac Hotel ✅ B → Troy Ancient City = B → Troy Ancient City ✅ A → 40.1490,26.4021 = A → Nearby gas station ❌
💡 QR code: Shows on your desktop screen. Point your phone camera at it — Google Maps opens automatically on your phone. No extension needed on mobile.

🏷️Layer types

📍 Places Layer
Pins dropped on the map — a list of spots you might visit. Order doesn't matter. Click any name to open its Google Maps info page. No navigation link is generated.
🛣️ Route Layer
Layers created with My Maps' "Add directions" tool. Contains ordered stops A, B, C... with a drawn path. Includes navigation link, travel mode selector, and stop management.

How to create a route layer

1
In My Maps, click + Add layer
2
Give the layer a name
3
Select Add directions (arrow icon) from the toolbar
4
Choose travel mode → add stops in order

📤Exports

🌐 HTML Export
Opens your full trip plan as an interactive page in a new tab.

• SVG route preview (🟢 start, 🔴 end, waypoints marked)
• Total and per-segment distances
• Stop list with notes
• Clickable Google Maps, Apple Maps, Waze buttons
• Reorder stops and change travel mode directly on the page

Use "Save Page As" to keep an offline .html copy.
📝 Markdown Export
Downloads an Obsidian / Notion / Logseq compatible .md file with YAML frontmatter, stop list, distances, notes, and navigation links.
📅 iCal Export
Downloads a .ics file. Each stop becomes a calendar event with coordinates and your note in the description. Dates default to today — move them after importing. Compatible with Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, and Outlook.

📝Stop notes

Every stop and place has a 📝 icon button. Click it to open an inline input (max 100 characters). Press Enter or Escape to close. Stops with a note show a highlighted icon. Hover over any name to see its note as a tooltip.

💾 Notes are saved with chrome.storage.local, grouped by map ID. They survive popup close, browser restart, and system reboot. Cleared only when the extension is uninstalled.

Notes appear in all exports:

HTML export: 📝 Allow 2 hours here (italic purple) MD export: 1. 🟢 **Grand Anzac Hotel** *(Breakfast included, leave by 09:00)* iCal: Shown in the DESCRIPTION field of the calendar event

📏Distance calculation

Distances are calculated with the Haversine formula — fully local, no API key needed.

⚠️ These are crow-flies distances. Actual road distance is typically 20–40% longer. Use these figures as a rough guide only.
● Grand Anzac Hotel ↓ 8.2 km ● Troy Ancient City ↓ 12.7 km ● Kepez Public Park 📏 Total: 20.9 km (crow-flies — actual road may be 20–40% longer)

❓FAQ

My map is private — will it work?
Yes. The extension uses your existing browser session. If you can see the map in your browser, the extension can read it.
Does reordering stops change my original My Maps?
No. Changes are local to the extension and the generated link only. Your original map is never modified.
Where are my notes stored?
In chrome.storage.local — only on your computer, in your browser profile. Notes for different maps are kept separate. Removed only when you uninstall the extension.
Can I use this on mobile?
No, extensions only work in desktop Chrome. Use the extension on desktop, then send the links to your phone or scan the QR code.
Google Maps opens the wrong place — what do I do?
Use the full, specific place name in My Maps (e.g. "Troy Ancient City Museum" rather than just "Museum"). Vague names may match the wrong location.
How do I update the extension?
Download the new ZIP and replace the folder contents. Then go to chrome://extensions and remove and re-add the extension for new permissions to take effect.
