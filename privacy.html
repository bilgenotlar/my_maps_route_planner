Privacy Policy

My Maps Route Planner — Chrome Extension
Last updated: March 2026

Short version: This extension does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything runs locally in your browser. No servers, no tracking, no analytics.

📋 Overview

My Maps Route Planner ("the Extension") is a Chrome browser extension that reads map data from Google My Maps pages you have open in your browser and generates navigation links for Google Maps, Apple Maps, and Waze.

This Privacy Policy explains what data the Extension accesses, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

📦 Data We Access

Data Purpose Stored?
Active tab URL Detect if the current page is a Google My Maps map (checks for google.com/maps/d/) No
Map KML data Read layer names, place names and coordinates from the open My Maps page to generate navigation links No — only held in memory while popup is open
Map page title Display the map name in the extension popup header No
Stop notes Optional short notes (max 100 characters) added by the user for each stop or place Yes — locally in chrome.storage.local only
Layer & route data Temporarily written to chrome.storage.local to pass data to the export page Temporary — overwritten on next export

🔒 Data Storage

The Extension uses chrome.storage.local exclusively for two purposes:

  • User notes: Notes you write for stops and places are saved locally so they persist between browser sessions. They are keyed by map ID (mid) and never leave your device.
  • Export handoff: When you open the HTML export page, map data is temporarily stored in local storage so the export page can read it. This data is overwritten each time you export and is never transmitted anywhere.

All data stored by the Extension exists solely on your device and is automatically removed when you uninstall the Extension.

🚫 Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect any personally identifiable information
  • We do not transmit any data to external servers
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting, or telemetry
  • We do not access your Google account credentials or authentication tokens
  • We do not store map data beyond the current session (except temporary export handoff)
  • We do not share any data with third parties

🌐 Third-Party Services

When you use the Extension's navigation buttons, links open in your browser pointing to third-party services. The Extension does not control and is not responsible for the privacy practices of:

  • Google Maps (maps.google.com) — subject to Google's Privacy Policy
  • Apple Maps (maps.apple.com) — subject to Apple's Privacy Policy
  • Waze (waze.com) — subject to Waze's Privacy Policy
  • WhatsApp (wa.me) — subject to Meta's Privacy Policy

The Extension reads KML data from google.com/maps/d/ using your existing browser session. This is the same request your browser would make when viewing the map — no additional credentials are accessed or stored.

🔑 Permissions Explained

Permission Why it's needed
activeTab Read the URL and title of the currently active tab to detect My Maps pages and show the map name
scripting Run a script in the My Maps tab to fetch the map's KML data using the browser's existing session credentials
clipboardWrite Copy navigation links to clipboard when you click the Copy button
downloads Save exported Markdown (.md) and iCalendar (.ics) files to your computer
storage Save your stop notes locally so they persist between sessions
Host: google.com/maps/d/* Fetch KML data from the My Maps page you have open. Access is limited to My Maps URLs only.

👤 Your Rights & Control

  • Delete notes: Clear notes for any map by emptying the note field. All notes are removed when you uninstall the Extension.
  • Inspect stored data: Open Chrome DevTools → Application → Storage → Extension Storage to view everything the Extension has stored.
  • Remove all data: Uninstalling the Extension permanently removes all locally stored data.

👶 Children's Privacy

This Extension is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.
